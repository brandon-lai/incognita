# Terra Incognita

## Atmosphere
A field atlas opened at night. The globe is the illustration; the controls are slips of paper laid beside it. Keep the fog, small ships, source notes and the distinction between documented tracks and reconstructions.

## Palette and type
Use the roles in `app/paper.css`: night `#120d09` behind the globe; parchment `#e6d6b0` and its light and dark variants for paper; ink `#2a1c11` for text; route red `#8f2a17` for the track and draft stamps. IM Fell English is for titles and journal prose, its small caps for chart labels; system sans is for explanations and numbers. All fonts are served locally.

## Components and layout
Paper corners are 2–4px, with a hairline and a soft shadow. Only the timeline and round icon buttons use fully rounded ends. The original date ranges, letterpressed stamps, translucent map overlays and illustration-specific color literals are intentional. Shadcn/Base UI supplies control behavior without Tailwind preflight. The full-screen globe and horizontal voyage shelf are deliberate; the text edition uses normal document flow and works without scripting.
